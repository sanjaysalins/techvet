## 10. Disagreement with prior fixes

The round-2-through-5 fix sequence is **defensibly prioritized by frequency** — DS/SA/Security/DevOps personas dominated the simulation cast for rounds 2-5, so fixes naturally clustered there. But the priority logic implicitly **discounted Mobile because no Mobile session ran**, not because Mobile was healthier. The round-1 Alex Tan findings (iOS-specialist false-flattery, no Compose/SwiftUI, no Mobile-specific methodology) are **all still open in round 6** — Bug-5 closed only the worst sub-case of the first one. **5 rounds without Mobile re-validation = 5 rounds of priority list compounding interest against Mobile**. Round 6 confirms this. The Mobile preload list, the methodology absence, the catalog gap, and the Bug-4 batch-enrich mismatch are not regressions from the round-2-5 fixes; they're **untouched pre-existing issues that look worse by comparison now that every other template has been polished**.

The narrowest disagreement: when 5ι shipped with the "3-card vs 4-card grid" justification, the design choice to **hide the entire Methodology section on Assessment when chips=[] AND entries=[]** (per `Assessment.tsx:408`) was correct *for Custom template* but accidentally regressive for **Mobile, Frontend, and Backend** templates which have no chips. Those three templates have legitimate methodology surfaces; hiding the section means the recruiter never discovers free-text capture exists. A minimal fix: render an empty Methodology section with a free-text input and a "no template chips for this role — type to add" hint, **always**. Effort: 5 lines in `Assessment.tsx`. This would not have been catchable without the Mobile (or Frontend, or Backend-without-chips) session — proof that round 6's "extend coverage to untested templates" theme is doing real work.
